We attended the IEEE International Symposium on Circuits and Systems (ISCAS) 2026 and presented a live demonstration of our plantar insole platform.

![ISCAS 2026 insole demo]( /images/conference/2026iscas_shanghai_demo.jpg)

Photo: Dr. Yu Wu (center) with Junjian Chi and collaborators during the ISCAS 2026 demo. Thank you to Dr. Yu Wu and our collaborators for their cooperation.

At the conference we showcased interactive insole-based mini-games that let visitors try real-time pressure-driven controls and gesture interactions using our high-resolution plantar sensing system. The demo highlighted the insole's crosstalk compensation and low-latency readout, and attracted strong interest from attendees and students.
